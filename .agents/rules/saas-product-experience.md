# SaaS Product Experience Rule

Apply when planning or implementing public pages/launch positioning, localization, acquisition measurement, accounts, roles/admin, onboarding, trials/billing, UX/accessibility, notifications/reports/support, or export/closure. Read [the reference map](../references/saas-product-experience.md) and relevant canonical plan sections first.

- Treat public trial/paid launch as an end-to-end milestone, independent of later scale work. Internal SEO validation may precede it; the documentation checkpoint does not start application implementation.
- Preserve open commercial choices. Do not invent seven versus thirty days, card/renewal policy, prices, allowances, or retention periods; resolve and record them before public offers activate.
- Grant trials only after verified-email eligible organization creation, once per eligible organization with durable idempotency. Invitations, callback retries, projects, and ownership changes must not reset the trial. Automatic activation is not consent to undisclosed charges.
- Enforce the canonical role/action matrix and entitlements separately in the backend, scheduler, and workers. Preserve last-owner invariants, scoped billing/exports, revocation, staff MFA, and audited administrative corrections.
- Use maintained account/recovery components, non-enumerating reset responses, expiring single-use tokens, verified email changes, session revocation, and trusted return/link origins. Do not log verification or reset secrets.
- Use supported hosted payment flows; grant paid access only from verified/reconciled server-side state. Handle signed webhook replay, duplicates, out-of-order events, and atomic quota reservation/settlement. Trial expiry must stop costly work even if a reminder or scheduler is delayed.
- Keep cancellation, expired/read-only access, suspended accounts, retention, and deletion distinct. Preserve allowed recovery/export routes and clearly explain downgrade, renewal, and closure consequences.
- Keep public marketing discoverable and truthful; protect/non-index private account/dashboard/report surfaces. Demo data must be labeled and isolated. Marketing and checkout must match actual capabilities and entitlements.
- Before final public copy/wireframes, require an accepted audience/value proposition and reviewed page/keyword/CTA map. Preserve unresolved URLs, markets, languages, providers, and content/analytics choices rather than silently selecting them.
- Give public content/proof an owner and preview/review/build/rollback workflow. Case studies need measured evidence and publication permission; product localization must match supported public/app/email/support coverage and valid published locale URLs.
- Define acquisition events by visitor/user/organization grain, deduplication, authoritative conversion state, attribution/cohort windows, and consent limits. Keep product acquisition analytics separate from customer website data and exclude secrets or customer exports from event properties.
- Validate advertised provider access/approval, capabilities, quotas/costs, and terms before launch claims; D103 lets code and checked configuration UI precede those real-provider checks without claiming availability. Verify the product site's own SEO and funnel at the real deployment, with post-launch ownership/cadence (plan Section 34.2).
- Public landing/marketing pages use semantic Angular HTML and custom native CSS, without PrimeNG or other UI/CSS frameworks. Prerender indexable public routes at build time; account/app routes use CSR, and production frontend hosting must not require Node or SSR. Scope authenticated UI dependencies/themes away from public pages.
- Validate the static route inventory, content/metadata in generated HTML, real public 404/HTTP redirects, app-only CSR fallback, asset budgets, and refresh of every generated HTML page on deployment (plan Sections 23.7 and 25.2).
- Use progressive resumable onboarding, plain-language actions with inspectable evidence, consistent components, and explicit empty/error/partial/stale/quota/expiry states. Target WCAG 2.2 AA and validate core journeys; missing data is not a passing result.
- Recheck recipient/share permissions, deduplicate notifications, keep report artifacts private, and support expiring/revocable external shares only with authorized scope. Provide safe support identifiers and disclosed export/closure/retention behavior.

The plan is authoritative; this rule is a review constraint, not a duplicate product specification. For AI-visibility claims or metrics, also follow the SEO data rule.
