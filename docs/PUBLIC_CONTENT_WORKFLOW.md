### Expiry cleanup and monitoring (D152)

The default worker's `tenancy.purge_public_analytics` task is scheduled every hour, independently of the collection enable switch/policy, so disabling intake cannot disable erasure. It drains500-epoch batches, at most100 batches or25seconds per invocation, and schedules an argument-free continuation after one second when expired work remains (including rows temporarily locked by other transactions). Late-ack/worker-loss redelivery can repeat this idempotent erasure; no collection/proof/customer arguments are queued. This is a75-minute-from-issuance **healthy-operation target**, not proof of an actual hosted deadline. Monitor Beat singleton health, default-worker queue/health and continuation failures; the staff-MFA public-observations view reports only `expired_pending`/`deadline_missed` flags. Alert on categorical `public_analytics.cleanup_overdue` (checked both before and after drain) and `public_analytics.cleanup_dispatch_failed`, without durable event-count rollups or row/proof logging. A queued/locked/stalled cleanup or missing scheduler must be treated as missed retention readiness, not a passing metric.
